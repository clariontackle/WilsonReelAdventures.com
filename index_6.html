Your Captain

Capt. Bill Wilson

I was born and raised in Ruskin, and I'm the fourth generation of my family to call it home. I grew up fishing Tampa Bay and I've been on these waters my whole life.

I served 17 years as a Deputy Sheriff with Manatee County, and in February 2022 I was blessed by the Lord to be able to retire.

Today my wife and I own and operate Wilson's Animal Removal and Wilson's Fencing, and now Wilson's Reel Adventures, so I can share the bay I grew up on with you. We have six beautiful children, and we attend and serve at The River Church of Tampa Bay.

When you step on my boat, you're fishing with someone who knows this bay like his backyard. Whether it's your first time holding a rod or you've been chasing fish for years, I'll build the trip around you and your crew.
